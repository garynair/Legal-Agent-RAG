## Summaries

### 11 — India GCC Recruitment Process Outsourcing Agreement
BluePeacock Talent Solutions runs recruitment for Northstar Vector Systems Inc.'s Bengaluru GCC, targeting 180 hires (software/cloud, data/analytics, finance, people-ops roles). Covers sourcing, requisition SLAs by role class (7–20 business days for initial slate), candidate-ownership/duplicate rules, and a 90-day replacement guarantee (free replacement search, credit if unfilled after 60 days). Monthly management fee INR 600,000 plus success fees per hire (INR 55,000–90,000, or 10% of comp for Director+). Meridian Harbor GCC Advisory is a signatory as GCC Adviser but assumes no employer/guarantor liability. Governing law: Karnataka; arbitration in Bengaluru.

### 12 — India GCC Staffing and Contract Personnel Agreement
DeccanBridge Staffing Services supplies and employs contract personnel (software engineers, test automation, finance ops, program support) assigned to Alderwave Financial Technologies plc's Hyderabad GCC, forecast at 65 personnel. Supplier is sole legal employer; rate card ranges INR 135,000–350,000/month by role. Covers overtime rules, timesheet approval, permanent-conversion fees (12%/6%/0% by month of conversion), and statutory-compliance certification. 24-month initial term, auto-renewing 12-month periods; highest contract value in this category (INR 48,600,000) and high risk given large-scale employment-liability exposure. Governing law: Telangana; arbitration in Hyderabad.

### 13 — India Interim Employer of Record Services Agreement
SaffronOrbit Employment Services acts as interim legal employer for up to 28 India-based personnel on behalf of Fjordlight Robotics AS (Norway) pending incorporation of "Fjordlight Robotics India Private Limited." Covers payroll pre-funding requirements, security deposit (INR 4.2M), benefits, permanent-establishment tax disclaimers, and a defined migration path to the future Indian entity (consensual resignation/rehire, INR 20,000/employee migration fee). Initial period ends 9 October 2026, extendable month-to-month up to 3 months at a 10% fee premium. High risk given employment/PE tax exposure. Governing law: Maharashtra; arbitration in Pune.

### 14 — GCC Payroll and Benefits Administration Agreement
CoralLedger People Operations administers payroll and benefits for Seabrook Clinical Data Pte. Ltd.'s Chennai workforce (145 employees, forecast to 320). Covers the monthly payroll calendar (input cutoffs, preliminary/final files), statutory-deduction calculations, benefits enrollment coordination, and payroll-error correction SLAs (99.7% accuracy target). Three-year initial term, auto-renewing, INR 13,500,000 indicative value (INR 275,000/month base + per-head fees above 200 employees). Governing law: Tamil Nadu; arbitration in Chennai.

### 15 — India GCC Candidate Background Verification Services Agreement
VerityMango Screening conducts consent-based pre-employment/employment background checks for Rheinspire Mobility GmbH's Gurugram GCC, offering four packages (Standard/Enhanced/Finance Trust/Executive, INR 3,800–12,500 per candidate). Covers candidate-consent requirements, turnaround-time targets (10–12 business days for full reports), Clear/Review/Adverse Information/Insufficient classification, adverse-finding dispute process, and strict data-retention limits (180-day report retention). 12-month term, auto-renewing, INR 4,200,000 estimated annual value. Governing law: Haryana; arbitration in Gurugram.
