# Category: Workplace, Technology & Operational Vendors

These are the physical and technology infrastructure vendors that make a GCC location operational.

Use this folder when the question is about: office space (managed workspace vs. leased office), fit-out/construction coordination, hardware procurement (laptops, network gear), IT service desk/support SLAs, or facilities/security staffing and incident response.

## Summaries

### 16 — Managed Workspace Agreement — Bengaluru Delivery Centre
Cedar Square Workspaces provides a ready-to-occupy managed office (200 workstations, seventh/eighth floors) for Northstar Clinical Systems India Private Limited's Bengaluru healthcare-tech GCC. 36-month term with a 24-month lock-in (early exit during lock-in incurs a charge for the unexpired period). Includes furniture, utilities, housekeeping, shared 1 Gbps internet (non-dedicated), and business-continuity provisions (alternate space for up to 40 seats if unusable >5 days). Monthly fee INR 3,300,000, 6% annual escalation; security deposit INR 9,900,000. Highest-value contract of the "managed workspace" type. Governing law: India (Karnataka venue); arbitration in Bengaluru.

### 17 — Office Lease and Fit-Out Coordination Agreement — Pune Engineering Centre
Sahyadri Commercial Estates leases ~45,000 sq ft (floors 10–11, Horizon Forge Tower, Kharadi, Pune) to Velorum Mobility Technologies India Private Limited for a 5-year term (2026–2031) with a 3-year renewal option and 36-month lock-in. Base rent INR 2,700,000/month with 15% escalation every 36 months; security deposit INR 16,200,000. Meridian Harbor GCC Advisory acts as Fit-Out Coordinator (separate INR 4,800,000 fee, excluded from lease value) managing the tenant-funded fit-out. Highest contract value in this category (INR 189,000,000) and high risk given the long-term real-estate commitment and reinstatement obligations. Governing law: India (Maharashtra venue); arbitration in Pune.

### 18 — IT Infrastructure Procurement and Deployment Agreement — Chennai Product Hub
Blue Circuit Technologies procures, configures, and deploys end-user and network hardware (220 engineering laptops, 120 standard laptops, 680 monitors, network switches/firewalls, meeting-room kits) for LumenArc Retail Platforms India Private Limited's 320-person Chennai hub. One-year, non-renewing project contract with hard milestones tied to a 5 October 2026 first-cohort start date. Maximum value INR 68,450,000, milestone-based payment, delay credits, and a 3-year hardware warranty pass-through. High risk given time-critical delivery and security/counterfeit-goods exposure. Governing law: India (Tamil Nadu venue); arbitration in Chennai.

### 19 — Managed IT Support and Service Desk Agreement — Hyderabad Fintech GCC
NimbusDesk Digital Services provides 24x7 service desk and deskside support for up to 500 users at Argent Vale Payments India Private Limited's Hyderabad fintech GCC. Defines P1–P4 incident SLAs (P1: 10-min response/4-hr resolution), joiner-mover-leaver processing, endpoint/asset management, and service-credit mechanics (up to 15%/month, chronic-failure termination trigger). Three-year initial term, auto-renewing annually; maximum base value INR 93,600,000. High risk given fintech data sensitivity and 24x7 criticality. Governing law: India (Telangana venue); arbitration in Hyderabad.

### 20 — Integrated Facilities Management and Physical Security Agreement — Gurugram GCC
Sentinel Grove Facilities delivers housekeeping, maintenance, reception/visitor management, and physical security guarding for PrairieStone Agritech India Private Limited's 72,000 sq ft Gurugram GCC. Specifies minimum staffing by shift (security officers, technicians, housekeeping), Critical/High/Medium/Routine incident SLAs, CCTV/access-log retention rules, and business-continuity provisions. 36-month initial term, auto-renewing annually; INR 75,600,000 base value with 6% annual escalation. Governing law: India (Haryana venue); arbitration in Gurugram.
