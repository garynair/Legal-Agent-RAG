## Summaries

### 21 — Integrated GCC Managed Services Agreement
Meridian Harbor GCC Advisory (in an operating capacity, not just adviser) delivers bundled HR operations, talent acquisition coordination (120 hires/year), finance operations, procurement, company-secretarial coordination, workplace administration (240 seats), and IT coordination for Northstar Biologics Group Inc.'s Bengaluru GCC. Includes detailed Schedule 1 baseline volumes and Schedule 2 critical service levels with tiered service credits (up to 10%/month). 36-month term, auto-renewing annually; fixed fee INR 1,900,000/month (INR 68,400,000 indicative total). High risk given the broad multi-function scope. Governing law: India; arbitration in Bengaluru.

### 22 — Finance and Accounting Managed Services Agreement
BlueLedger GCC Operations provides accounts-payable/receivable processing, reconciliations, fixed-asset accounting, month-end close coordination, and audit support for Caldera Mobility GmbH's Pune GCC, while the Client's CFO retains all statutory/fiduciary decisions. Strong segregation-of-duties controls (no self-approval on vendor master changes, independent bank-detail-change verification, fraud-freeze escalation within 30 minutes). 24-month fixed term (no auto-renewal), EUR 30,000/month (EUR 720,000 total). High risk given financial-control and fraud exposure. Governing law: India; arbitration in Mumbai.

### 23 — Procurement and Vendor Management Agreement
SourceBridge GCC Services operates procurement intake, sourcing, vendor due-diligence, and purchase-order administration for HelioWave Communications Pte. Ltd.'s Hyderabad GCC, covering indirect procurement (IT, facilities, professional services, travel). Defines tiered competitive-bidding thresholds (single quote up to INR 250K; Procurement Council approval above INR 10M) and strict anti-corruption/conflict-of-interest rules (no vendor gifts >INR 2,500). 24-month term, auto-renewing annually; INR 900,000/month (INR 21,600,000 total). Governing law: India; arbitration in Hyderabad.

### 24 — GCC Data Analytics and Performance Reporting Agreement
MetricSpring Analytics Services builds and operates executive dashboards (headcount, cost, service performance) for Alder & Finch Retail plc's Chennai GCC, integrating Workday, Greenhouse, Oracle Fusion, and ServiceNow. Includes a metric-governance council, data-quality thresholds (0.5%–1.0% variance tolerance), privacy-by-design suppression rules (<7 individuals), and explicit AI-governance restrictions (no predictive attrition scoring, no individual productivity ranking, no training on Client data). 24-month fixed term, no automatic renewal; GBP 78,000 implementation + GBP 15,750/month (GBP 456,000 total). Governing law: India; arbitration in Chennai.

### 25 — GCC Transition and Exit Management Agreement
HarborPath Transition Services manages the orderly wind-down/handover of VelaNova Energy S.A.'s 310-person Gurugram GCC from an incumbent build-operate arrangement to VelaNova's own entity and successor vendors. Covers employee transition, knowledge transfer (teach-observe-perform-validate model), technology/data migration, asset handover, vendor novation, parallel-run/cutover criteria, and post-cutover stabilization. Short, intense 6-month term (Aug 2026 – Jan 2027), non-renewing; fixed fee EUR 390,000 across five milestones. High risk given the complexity and time-pressure of a full operational cutover. Governing law: India; arbitration in New Delhi.
