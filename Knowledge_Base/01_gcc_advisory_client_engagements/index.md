Use this folder when the question is about: feasibility studies, city/location comparisons, GCC operating-model design, program governance during setup, build-operate-transfer (BOT) structures, or capability-maturity/expansion consulting.

## Summaries

### 01 — GCC Establishment Advisory Agreement
Meridian Harbor GCC Advisory advises Northstar Quill Software Corporation (Seattle-based) on establishing a Bengaluru GCC starting at 120 employees, scaling to 300 within 36 months. Scope: feasibility/business case, location scoring across Bengaluru/Hyderabad/Pune/Chennai, operating-model design, and a 90-day implementation roadmap. Purely advisory — excludes legal/tax opinions, incorporation, hiring, and lease brokerage. Fixed fee INR 9.6M in five milestone payments. Client termination for convenience on 20 days' notice. Governing law: Karnataka; arbitration in Bengaluru.

### 02 — Build-Operate-Transfer Agreement
Blue Banyan GCC Ventures builds, operates, and transfers a 250-person Hyderabad GCC for Fablebridge Payments plc (UK payments company), ramping to 250 personnel by June 2028 with transfer by 31 March 2029. Covers build phase (program office, workspace, control framework), operate phase (recruitment, payroll, IT, finance), and a defined transfer process (readiness criteria, employee offers, asset/contract transfer, 60-day stabilization support). Total value GBP 4.85M across build fee, monthly management fees, and transfer fee. High risk given multi-year employee/asset transfer complexity. Governing law: Telangana; arbitration in Hyderabad (3 arbitrators).

### 03 — GCC Program Management Agreement
Cedar Arc India Advisory provides PMO services to Eisenmere Motion Systems GmbH (German automotive engineering) for establishing a 175-person Pune engineering/digital-ops GCC, targeting soft launch (75 staff) Nov 2026 and full launch (125+ staff) Feb 2027. PMO coordinates but does not replace legal/construction/recruiting vendors; maintains integrated plan, risk/decision registers, vendor coordination, and launch-readiness reviews. Fixed fee EUR 1.32M paid in 12 monthly installments. Client can terminate for convenience on 30 days' notice plus demobilization fee. Governing law: Maharashtra; arbitration in Pune.

### 04 — India Market Entry and GCC Location Strategy Agreement
Kestrel Bridge Market Entry Advisors compares five Indian cities (Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune, Chennai, Gurugram) for Orchard Lantern Robotics Pte. Ltd. (Singapore robotics company) planning a ~65-person GCC scaling to 220 within four years. Deliverables: requirements memo, five-city fact pack, talent/compensation benchmark, real-estate/cost model, weighted scorecard, and final recommendation naming a preferred and contingency city. Explicitly excludes incorporation, site brokerage, hiring, and lease negotiation. Fixed fee SGD 410,000 in four milestone payments. Lowest risk-rated contract in this category. Governing law: Delhi; arbitration in New Delhi.

### 05 — GCC Expansion and Capability Transformation Consulting Agreement
Saffron Current Transformation Partners helps Caldera Grove Consumer Health S.A. (Swiss consumer-health company) expand its existing ~400-person Chennai GCC to ~750 employees by December 2028, shifting toward higher-value work (product data, digital commerce, clinical systems, analytics, automation/AI). Covers maturity assessment, capability-portfolio recommendations, future operating model, migration-wave roadmap, automation/AI opportunity assessment (with explicit prohibition on deploying production AI or making automated employment/medical decisions), and change management. Fixed fee USD 1.85M across six milestones, with an optional six-month implementation extension (capped at USD 720,000). Governing law: Tamil Nadu; arbitration in Chennai.
